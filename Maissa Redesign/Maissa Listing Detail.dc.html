The house

Four streets back from the Houmt Souk fish market, the villa is built around a courtyard that the original vaulted rooms open onto. The street door gives nothing away.

Thick walls and shuttered windows keep the rooms cool through the summer without air conditioning, though there is air conditioning in both bedrooms for August. Breakfast happens under the fig tree.

The market, the ferry terminal and the old town are all within a fifteen-minute walk. The east-coast beaches are a ten-minute drive.
